Information & Consent Form

Welcome to this empirical research study investigating user experience and content presentation in digital news mobile applications.

Study Overview & Purpose

Purpose of the Study: The aim of this research is to understand how readers interact with modern mobile news applications and how different interfaces affect user engagement, comprehension, and experience.


Procedure: If you agree to participate, you will:
1. Complete a brief baseline questionnaire about your general news reading habits (~2 minutes).
2. Explore a simulated mobile news application interface containing recent journalism across technology, science, world events, and culture (~3 minutes).
3. Complete a post-study questionnaire regarding your impressions and user experience (~3 minutes).


Voluntary Participation & Confidentiality: Your participation is completely voluntary. You may withdraw at any moment without penalty. All data collected (reading times, article views, responses) is strictly anonymized, associated solely with your unique Prolific Participant ID, and stored securely for scientific analysis in accordance with GDPR regulations. No personally identifiable information (PII) is recorded.


Compensation: Upon completing all steps, you will receive a Prolific completion code and be redirected back to Prolific to claim your reward.

Pre-Study Questionnaire

Please answer the following questions regarding your regular habits when reading news.

1. How often do you read or browse online news?

2. Which device do you primarily use to read news?

3. What is your primary source for discovering news articles?

News App Exploration Phase

News App Exploration Instructions

You are now about to interact with a prototype mobile news application called The Gazette.

Your Task

  • Freely explore the news feed as you normally would on your phone. You can scroll through the news using your mouse or trackpad.
  • Look at headlines, headers, and previews, and click on any articles that catch your interest to read them.
  • You can return to the main feed at any time using the back button.
  • Please spend at least 2 minutes exploring the interface. An exploration timer at the top will indicate your progress.
