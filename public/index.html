Study Overview & Purpose

Purpose of the Study: The aim of this experiment is to understand how readers interact with a fictive news app, named The Gazette.


Procedure: During the experiment, you will:
1. Complete a brief pre-study questionnaire regarding your preferences and news habits.
2. Freely explore The Gazette.
3. Complete a post-study questionnaire regarding your impressions and user experience.


Voluntary Participation & Confidentiality: Your participation is completely voluntary. You may withdraw at any moment without penalty. All data collected (reading times, article views, responses) is strictly anonymized, associated solely with your unique Prolific Participant ID, and stored securely for scientific analysis in accordance with GDPR regulations.
